WILDLANDS ONLINE

A 3D survival adventure

Explore six islands, including Hearthstone Village, with forest, meadow, mining and snowy habitats. Craft a boat, fish for food, mine ores and use stronger swords.

Public worlds will appear here when the server is running.

Share the world name and password with friends for private worlds.

Private world: play solo without connecting to a server.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to gather or attack, E to interact, C to craft a campfire. On iPad, use the movement pad on the lower left and action buttons on the lower right; the backpack stays above them. Tap Sword / Hit to attack. Craft a fishing rod and boat in your backpack, fish near shore, mine ore, then sail to other islands.

Online play requires this Node.js server to be running. Public rooms are listed; private rooms require the password. This is an evolving prototype; some multiplayer features are still experimental.
