WILDLANDS ONLINE

A 3D survival adventure prototype

Wake up on an untamed island. Gather resources, survive the wilds, and prepare a camp before nightfall.

Public worlds will appear here when the server is running.

Share the world name and password with friends for private worlds.

Private world: play solo without connecting to a server.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to gather or attack, E to interact, C to craft a campfire. On touchscreens, use the on-screen controls.

Online play requires this Node.js server to be running. Public rooms are listed; private rooms require the password. PvP and server-side saves are enabled in this build.
